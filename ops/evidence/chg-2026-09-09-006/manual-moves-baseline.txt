3. ⚠️ BUT `event` CHANGES ON BOTH PATHS — HA's own commands and the remote's alike. An

   could swallow the safety valve.
   THE DISCRIMINATOR IS CONTEXT: an automation-originated service call carries
   context.parent_id and a trigger; a manual or remote change has parent_id=null and
   user_id=null. Themis used exactly this to prove the natural 08:30 run in
   chg-2026-09-09-004. To be confirmed against these three events before any override
   design relies on it.

4. assumed_state=true does NOT mean HA is blind on cover.screen — it observes RF. That
   reassurance does NOT transfer to cover.screen_2, which has never received an event.
